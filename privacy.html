Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

IFTA Lite is a free fuel tax worksheet from TruckTaxPro. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not ask you to sign up, and it does not collect your miles or fuel figures.

What you enter stays on your device

The miles, gallons, states and rates you type into the worksheet are processed in your browser or app. They are not sent to our servers. To remember your last entries, the app saves them in your device's local storage. You can clear them at any time with the "Clear all" button or by clearing the site's data in your browser or app settings.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number is required.
  • No location data is collected. The app does not use GPS.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers are used.

Third-party services

  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like most hosts, it may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and operation.
  • Fonts. The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your device contacts Google's font servers when the page loads.
  • Links to TruckTaxPro. If you tap the Form 2290 offer, you leave this app and go to trucktaxpro.com, which has its own privacy policy.

Tax figures are estimates

IFTA Lite helps you compile figures for your IFTA return. It does not file a return or give tax advice. Check every number against your base-state tax portal and your own records before you submit.

Children

The app is intended for commercial drivers and carriers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If the app starts collecting new information, for example if accounts or reminders are added, we will update this page before that change goes live.
